WHEN IS THIS USEFUL?
Debug the payment request, not the merchant's intentions.


When an x402 client fails, start by checking the protocol version, accepted network, token contract, atomic amount and recipient format. A quote may be correctly formed even if the vendor is offline, the wallet cannot sign that asset, the facilitator is unavailable, or the service is dishonest. These are separate problems requiring separate verification.
